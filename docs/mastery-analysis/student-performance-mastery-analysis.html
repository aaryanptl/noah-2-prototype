What stands out

Scores in this report are not interchangeable: stored mastery is shown in section 01. Other sections calculate answer accuracy, with partial responses counted as not correct and non-attempts excluded. Question-weighted comparisons give longer tests more weight. No database scores have been changed.

Reading the evidence

Completed means a completion timestamp exists. Analyses of performance use completed sessions, except the speed overview, which includes all scored submissions. First response means the earliest submission by timestamp and ID; a first non-attempt is not replaced with a later answer. Missing scores remain missing.

Fast-answer thresholds are exploratory review flags. Recorded times may include idle time or instrumentation errors. Changes after practice are observational and do not establish that practice caused improvement. Student groups refer to subject, test grade and flow—not demographic groups.

Questions are linked through their served-session topic, objective, Bloom level and difficulty. Current taxonomy labels and current mastery rollups are used. No minimum evidence gate is applied to stored mastery distribution; comparison tables state their own gates. Students and sessions can appear in multiple topic groups.
