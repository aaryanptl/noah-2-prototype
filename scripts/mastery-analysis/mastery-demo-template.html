How to explain this comparison in the call

“The left side is the student’s actual history. The right side proposes the next action using evidence available at that moment. Higher scores after retries help us see progress within the activity; a fresh independent check is still needed before deciding the skill is secure.”

These are exploratory rules for mentor review: fewer than 5 answered questions across 2 sessions means limited evidence; under 60% suggests teaching; 60–79% suggests targeted practice; 80% or above suggests a fresh independent check. Repeated low performance suggests mentor review. These thresholds have not been validated as mastery cutoffs.

Only objectives represented in the selected tests appear. Missing objectives remain unknown. Different difficulty mixes and repeated questions can affect accuracy. First-response accuracy excludes a question if its first submission was non_attempt; final accuracy uses its last response. Previously served counts use question IDs and recorded serve times across all available flows; similar question variants may still go undetected.
